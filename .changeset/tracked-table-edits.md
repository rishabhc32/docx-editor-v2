In suggesting mode, inserting or deleting table columns, deleting a table or a selection that covers one, resizing rows and columns, and changing cell fill, vertical alignment, or borders now create tracked changes that you can accept or reject, as in Word. Review cards name proposed columns, the No Markup, Simple Markup, and Original views lay them out as accepting or rejecting them would, and rejecting a proposed table also removes its author's own edits inside it.
